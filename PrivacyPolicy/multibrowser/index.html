MultiBrowser — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

This privacy policy applies to the MultiBrowser app (package com.d0gu.multibrowser), a free, privacy-focused web browser.

1. Data we collect: none

MultiBrowser has no accounts and no analytics. The app does not send any personal data to the developer. Nothing you browse is seen, collected or stored by the developer.

2. Data stored on your device

The following is stored only in the app's private on-device storage and never leaves your device:

  • The profiles you create and their settings (device, language, time zone, proxy details)
  • Cookies, sessions and site data (per profile, for browsing to work)
  • Bookmarks and browsing history
  • Any site logins you choose to save

Proxy passwords and saved logins are stored encrypted in the device's secure keystore.

3. Backups

You can export your profiles to a file and choose where to send it. If you include proxy passwords in an export, treat that file as sensitive.

4. Network

Page traffic goes only to the websites you visit and, if configured, through your proxy. The app itself sends your data nowhere else, and contains no third-party ads, analytics or tracking SDKs.

5. Permissions

  • Internet access: used to load web pages.
  • Biometric / device credential: used only if you enable the optional app lock, and handled entirely by the operating system.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always published at this address.
